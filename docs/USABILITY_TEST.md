# Usability Test Kit

## Purpose

Observe whether students can complete five common study tasks with the Android app. This kit is a template for future peer/student sessions; it contains no fabricated participant observations.

## Session setup and ethical consent

- Invite participants voluntarily and explain the purpose, duration, and activities before starting.
- Do not collect names, passwords, MFA codes, real school records, or other unnecessary personal information.
- Use a test account and synthetic questions. Do not use a real Gemini credential or incur AI-provider charges during a session.
- Participants may stop or skip any task without penalty. Ask permission before taking notes, photos, screen recordings, or quotations.
- For any participant under 18, obtain guardian permission and the participant's assent before the session. For an under-13 participant, guardian permission is explicitly required before any participation or collection of data.
- Store completed sheets securely and remove them when no longer needed. Report only anonymous, aggregated findings.

## Moderator instructions

1. Read the consent information and confirm permission before opening the app.
2. Give the participant one task at a time. Avoid teaching the interface or suggesting the next action.
3. Record what the participant does and says, including errors and recovery, without capturing credentials.
4. Stop a task if the participant becomes uncomfortable. Ask a neutral follow-up after the task.

## Core tasks

1. **Register and onboard:** Create a test account and complete the onboarding steps.
2. **Ask a text question:** Ask an academic question and find the generated explanation.
3. **Ask a photo question:** Submit a synthetic worksheet image and find the explanation.
4. **Bookmark and find later:** Bookmark a question, leave the screen, and find it again in history/bookmarks.
5. **Take a practice quiz:** Start a practice quiz, answer it, submit it, and find the result.
